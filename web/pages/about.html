A community built for success

Bestlink College of the Philippines was founded in June 2002 as "LOG-ON TODAY" by Dr. Maria M. Vicente, with Engr. Reynaldo Corpuz as School Administrator.

Starting with just seven students, it grew from technical computer training into a full-fledged college, formally adopting the name Bestlink College of the Philippines in 2012.

Today the institution operates multiple campuses across Quezon City and Bulacan, serving a community of over 45,000 students with a comprehensive range of college, Senior High School, and TESDA-accredited programs.

Our Core

Our Mission

We provide and promote quality education with modern and unique techniques to enhance the skill and the knowledge of our dear students to make them globally competitive and productive citizens.

Our Vision

Bestlink College of the Philippines is committed to provide and promote quality education, with a unique, modern and research-based curriculum with delivery systems geared towards excellence.

Our Commitment

To provide accessible education to youth from marginalized families through scholarship programs, so deserving students pay only minimal miscellaneous fees.
